# Punny Boom 💥💬

Comic style double meaning meme generator! Boom goes the pun.

## Features
- **Auto Generate**: Instantly creates double-meaning memes on **viral templates** (Drake, Distracted Boyfriend, This is Fine, Success Kid, Futurama Fry, and more) via free memegen.link API. Shows the classic expression + your punny caption.
- **Upload Your Picture**: Overlay hilarious double-entendre captions on your own photo (canvas mode with watermark).
- **Comic UI**: Bold Bangers + Comic Neue fonts, thick borders, yellow comic paper vibe.
- **Export**: Download the meme (JPG for templates, PNG for uploads) with watermark on canvas mode.
- Fully client-side. No signup, no backend.

## SEO
Meta title, description, keywords, Open Graph, Twitter Card, and canonical URL.

## How to Use
1. Click **Auto Generate Fun Meme** → random viral template + double-meaning caption.
2. Or click **Upload Your Picture** to use your own photo.
3. Edit / regenerate as needed.
4. Hit **Export Meme**.
